# Frontend Design

> Source: https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design (Anthropic)
> Purpose: Guidance for distinctive, intentional visual design when building or refining frontend interfaces.

---

## Overview

Approach interface implementation as a design lead at a boutique studio known for giving every product a visual identity that could not be mistaken for anyone else's. Avoid templated, generic AI defaults: make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout that are specific to the brief, and maintain a distinctive point of view.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Ground it in the subject
- Name the concrete subject, its audience, and the page/component's single job.
- Leverage the product's own world, materials, instruments, artifacts, and vernacular to inspire design choices.
- Always design with real, contextual content rather than generic placeholder abstractions.

### 2. Design principles
- **Hero & Key Views as Thesis:** Open with the most characteristic element of the subject's world (live demo, interactive state, concise value proposition).
- **Typography Carries Personality:** Pair display and body faces intentionally, maintain a disciplined type scale, and ensure typography serves the product's identity.
- **Structure is Information:** Structural devices (dividers, labels, counters, indicators) should encode actual meaningful hierarchy or sequence, never arbitrary decoration.
- **Deliberate Motion:** Use animation with restraint and purpose (page-load sequence, hover micro-interactions, ambient live states). An orchestrated moment lands harder than scattered noisy effects.
- **Match Complexity to the Vision:** Precision in spacing, typography, and micro-details creates visual elegance.

### 3. Application to Design Systems
- When a project already possesses a defined Design System (such as NexusCord Hybrid), this skill reinforces the execution quality of that system rather than introducing conflicting exterior trends.
- Strictly adhere to established tokens, color tiers, border radiuses, and accessibility standards while elevating craft, hierarchy, and micro-interactions.
